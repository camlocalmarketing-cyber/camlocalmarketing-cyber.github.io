Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. The monthly plan is month-to-month. Cancel anytime from the billing page, and it stops at the end of the month you've paid for.

Will you post anything without asking me?

No. Every post and review reply is sent to you first, by text or email, whichever you prefer. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. You add me as a manager on your Google Business Profile, which takes 2 minutes, and you can remove me at any time.

Can you get me more reviews?

I'll help you ask real customers for reviews the right way, with a simple link and a QR code. I never write or buy fake reviews.

What's your refund policy?

Not happy in your first 30 days? Full refund. After that, cancel anytime and you won't be charged for any month that hasn't started. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days.
