Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No long contracts. Once your site is live, there's a 3-month minimum. After that, it's month-to-month: cancel anytime from the billing page, and it stops at the end of the month you've paid for. The Local Presence plan can be canceled anytime.

Will you post anything without asking me?

No. Every post and review reply is sent to you first, by text or email, whichever you prefer. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Do you need my Google password?

Never. You add me as a manager on your Google Business Profile, which takes 2 minutes, and you can remove me at any time.

Can you get me more reviews?

I'll help you ask real customers for reviews the right way, with a simple link and a QR code. I never write or buy fake reviews.

What if I want to keep my site after I cancel?

After 6 months with me, you can buy out your site for a one-time $400. You get the files, your photos and text, and simple instructions to host it yourself. Otherwise, your site comes down when service ends.

What's your refund policy?

Cancel before your site goes live and you get a full refund. Once it's live, your first 3 months are non-refundable. After that, there are no refunds, but you can cancel anytime: you keep service through the month you've paid for and aren't charged again. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days.
